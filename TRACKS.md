# Tracks — StellarX Philippines

Six tracks, built directly from the 13 idea categories in [`stellar-300-ideas.md`](./stellar-300-ideas.md). Each track tells you exactly which ideas belong to it, so you can go from "which track?" to "which idea?" in one step.

> Pick **one** track at submission. Your project is judged only in that track. See [`JUDGING.md`](./JUDGING.md) for scoring and [`SUBMISSION_GUIDELINES.md`](./SUBMISSION_GUIDELINES.md) for how to submit.

---

## How Tracks Map to the 300 Ideas

The [`stellar-300-ideas.md`](./stellar-300-ideas.md) file has 13 categories. Those 13 are consolidated into **6 tracks** so the competition stays focused:

| Track | Idea categories it covers | Idea numbers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Remittance & Cross-Border | Category 1 | 1–25 |
| 2 — Financial Inclusion & Everyday Payments | Categories 2 + 3 | 26–80 |
| 3 — DeFi, Stablecoins & Real-World Assets | Categories 4 + 7 | 81–110, 156–180 |
| 4 — AI-Powered Stellar Apps | Category 5 | 111–135 |
| 5 — Social Impact (Health, Education, Agriculture) | Categories 10 + 11 + 12 | 221–280 |
| 6 — Open Innovation (Gaming, Enterprise, Dev Tools, Novel) | Categories 6 + 8 + 9 + 13 | 136–155, 181–220, 281–300 |

You are **not limited** to the listed ideas — they are starting points. Any project that fits a track's theme is welcome. If your idea genuinely fits none of these, submit to **Track 6: Open Innovation**.

## Track 1 — Remittance & Cross-Border Payments

**The flagship track.** The Philippines receives $36B+ in annual remittances. This track is about moving value across borders faster, cheaper, and more transparently than SWIFT, banks, or legacy remittance operators.

**Build things like:** corridor rate aggregators, purpose-locked remittances, OFW savings tools, multi-recipient transfers, anchor failover routers, claimable-balance inheritance, FX-hedged transfers.

- **Idea bank:** [`stellar-300-ideas.md`](./stellar-300-ideas.md) → Category 1, ideas **1–25**
- **Key Stellar tools:** anchors (SEP-24/6/31), path payments, USDC, claimable balances
- **Strong fit if:** your product touches an OFW, a sender abroad, or a receiving family in the Philippines.

---

## Track 2 — Financial Inclusion & Everyday Payments

For the 70% of Filipinos who are underbanked or unbanked, and for the everyday commerce — sari-sari stores, market vendors, jeepney fares — that runs on cash today.

**Build things like:** no-KYC micro-savings, street vendor POS, sari-sari inventory finance, QR/tap-to-pay merchant tools, layaway, BNPL, transparent community funds, cooperative dividend engines.

- **Idea bank:** [`stellar-300-ideas.md`](./stellar-300-ideas.md) → Categories 2 + 3, ideas **26–80**
- **Key Stellar tools:** classic assets, sponsored reserves, multiplexed accounts, micropayments, multi-sig
- **Strong fit if:** your product brings someone into the financial system or replaces a cash workflow.

---

## Track 3 — DeFi, Stablecoins & Real-World Assets

On-chain finance and tokenized real-world value: lending, savings, yield, structured products, stablecoins, and tokenized assets like land, crops, equipment, and inventory.

**Build things like:** proof-of-reserve dashboards, fixed-rate lending, crop forward contracts, compliance-grade token issuance, tokenized warehouse receipts, fractional ownership, on-chain bonds.

- **Idea bank:** [`stellar-300-ideas.md`](./stellar-300-ideas.md) → Categories 4 + 7, ideas **81–110** and **156–180**
- **Key Stellar tools:** Soroban, Soroswap, Blend, Reflector oracle, clawback, asset flags, AMM pool shares
- **Strong fit if:** your product manages, grows, lends, or tokenizes value on-chain.

---

## Track 4 — AI-Powered Stellar Apps

Where AI meets Stellar. Products that use AI to make Stellar usable, safe, or autonomous — and AI agents that transact on Stellar rails.

**Build things like:** natural-language payment interfaces, AI credit scoring from on-chain data, fraud detection, sanctions screening, anchor uptime monitors, conversational DeFi, AI portfolio advisors.

- **Idea bank:** [`stellar-300-ideas.md`](./stellar-300-ideas.md) → Category 5, ideas **111–135**
- **Key Stellar tools:** Horizon data, Soroban, x402, the `stellar-dev` skill, MCP servers
- **Strong fit if:** AI is doing real work — not a chatbot bolted onto a wallet. See [`Recommended_AI_Tools.md`](./Recommended_AI_Tools.md).

---

## Track 5 — Social Impact (Health, Education, Agriculture)

Stellar as public infrastructure: transparent funds, verifiable credentials, parametric insurance, and direct disbursement for healthcare, education, and the agricultural communities that feed the country.

**Build things like:** transparent school/medical funds, scholarship and stipend disbursement, parametric crop insurance, telehealth micropayments, cold-chain breach logs, cooperative crop pricing, verified-hours credentials.

- **Idea bank:** [`stellar-300-ideas.md`](./stellar-300-ideas.md) → Categories 10 + 11 + 12, ideas **221–280**
- **Key Stellar tools:** multi-sig, Soroban conditional release, oracles, credentials, micropayments
- **Strong fit if:** your product helps a school, a clinic, a farmer, or a vulnerable community.

---

## Track 6 — Open Innovation (Gaming, Enterprise, Dev Tools, Novel)

The catch-all for everything else strong: gaming and NFTs, B2B and enterprise tooling, developer infrastructure, and novel ideas that don't fit a neat box.

**Build things like:** in-game asset economies, esports prize pools, supply chain finance, corporate treasury tools, claimable-balance UIs, SEP conformance testers, path payment visualizers, or anything genuinely new.

- **Idea bank:** [`stellar-300-ideas.md`](./stellar-300-ideas.md) → Categories 6 + 8 + 9 + 13, ideas **136–155**, **181–220**, **281–300**
- **Key Stellar tools:** anything — this track rewards creative use of the full Stellar toolkit
- **Strong fit if:** your idea is strong but doesn't fit Tracks 1–5, or it's developer tooling the ecosystem needs.

## Choosing Your Track — Quick Guide

```
Does it move money across a border?              → Track 1
Does it bank the underbanked or digitize cash?   → Track 2
Does it lend, save, yield, or tokenize value?    → Track 3
Is AI doing the heavy lifting?                   → Track 4
Does it help health, education, or agriculture?  → Track 5
Anything else strong (games, B2B, dev tools)?    → Track 6
```

If two tracks fit, pick the one where your **core flow** lives — not a secondary feature.

## Before You Commit to a Track

1. Open [`stellar-300-ideas.md`](./stellar-300-ideas.md) and read your track's idea range.
2. Check novelty: search `stellar_repos.txt` for your concept's keywords. 5+ direct matches? Sharpen your angle.
3. Scope it: what is the **smallest strong demo** of this idea? That's what you build.
4. Confirm Stellar is core — re-read criterion 1 in [`JUDGING.md`](./JUDGING.md).
