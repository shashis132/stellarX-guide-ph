## What This Hackathon Is About

The Philippines is one of the most important markets in the world for real crypto utility — a massive remittance corridor, a deeply digital consumer base, and millions of underbanked users. StellarX Philippines exists to turn that opportunity into working products.

You will build something real on Stellar — a wallet, a payment app, a remittance flow, a DeFi product, or a consumer financial experience — and demo it on testnet.

**The bar is not "most code." The bar is "strongest working demo of a real idea."**

## The Path From Zero to Submission

1. **Read** [`README.md`](./README.md) and [`Starter_prompts.md`](./Starter_prompts.md).
2. **Set up** your environment using [`dev_setup`](./dev_setup). Five minutes here saves hours.
3. **Pick a track** in [`TRACKS.md`](./TRACKS.md) and an idea from [`stellar-300-ideas.md`](./stellar-300-ideas.md).
4. **Build** — use [`stellar-fullstack-cheatsheet.md`](./stellar-fullstack-cheatsheet.md) as your scaffold. Use AI; this repo has [`Free_AI_Setup.md`](./Free_AI_Setup.md) if you need it.
5. **Test** your core flow on testnet end to end.
6. **Record** a 2–4 minute demo video.
7. **Submit** via GitHub Issue per [`SUBMISSION_GUIDELINES.md`](./SUBMISSION_GUIDELINES.md) before the deadline.

---

## Three Rules That Matter Most

1. **Build on Stellar, meaningfully.** Stellar must be core to the product, not a logo on a slide.
2. **Original work only.** No re-use of pre-existing projects, no plagiarism. See [`SUBMISSION_GUIDELINES.md`](./SUBMISSION_GUIDELINES.md).
3. **It must run.** A rough but working demo beats an ambitious idea with nothing to show.
